CHOCOLATE CAKE AND CUPCAKES  
Yields: 8” 2-layer Cake, 12 Cupcakes, 9"X13" Sheet Cake 
WAY better than a box mix. Only uses one bowl, no mixer required.  
Thank you for purchasing a fresh dry mix batch from our bakery!  
Follow these simple steps to bake bakery-quality treats at home.
---
**What You Need:**  
1 Package Chocolate Cake Mix.  
    Base Dry Mix includes: Flour, Sugars, Leavening, Salt, etc.  

**Equipment**  
* Hand Mixer & Medium mixing bowl or Stand Mixer  
* Spatula  
* Standard 8-inch round cake pans  
    To make cupcakes, use a standard cupcake pan (12) with cupcake liners.  
    To make Sheet Cake, use metal 9"X13" rectangle sheet pan
* Parchment paper (for cakes)  
---
**Wet Ingredients (To Add)** 
* 1/2 cup(1 stick, 113g) unsalted butter room temp   
    Or 1/4 cup + 3 T (87mL) vegetable oil (or amy neutral flavored oil)  
* 2 large eggs, room temperature  
* 1 cup (8 oz, 227mL) full-fat sour cream + 3 T water   
    Or use same amounts plain yogurt + water  
    Or 1 cup (227mL) whole milk, buttermilk, or unsweetened milk alternative (such as almond)    
* 1 teaspoon (5mL) pure vanilla extract (Old fashioned Homemade flavor)  
    Or 1 teaspoon (5mL) clear vanilla + 1/4 teaspoon almond extract (for that Bakery flavor) 
---
**Baking Instructions**
1. Prep:  
    Preheat oven to 350 F (175 C).  
**Layer cake:**  
    Prep two 8” cake pans by greasing sides and bottom with butter or unflavored vegetable spray and lining bottom with parchment paper. Grease or spray parchment.  
![alt text](how-to-line-a-round-pan-21-1.jpg)   
**Cupcakes:**  
    Line a standard cupcake pan (12) with cupcake liners (no butter or spray required).  
**9 x 13 in. Pan**  
    Preheat Oven to 300°F (150°C), spray pan with vegetable spray, line with parchment and spray again.  
    [Trace pan’s base onto parchment paper, extending the lines on each side  
    Cut out the corner squares making a “cross” shape  
    Fold the paper and fit to the bottom of the pan]   
![alt text](<How to line a rectangular pan.jpg>)
2. Mix   
In a mixer bowl, add eggs and mix until fluffy and pale yellow. about 2 minutes. Add buttermilk, oil, and vanilla. Mix well, about a minute.
3. Combine Wet Ingredients & Mix  
Pour the entire contents of the Chocolate Cake Dry Mix Pouch to a medium-sized the mixing.  
4. Add hot water to the mixture. Beat on low speed until smooth and just combined (no dry cake mix streaks). 
6. Be sure to scrape down the sides of the mixture as needed to make sure that everything is incorporated into the batter.
5. Bake  
**Layer cake:**  
7. Divide the batter evenly between the two prepped 8″ round cake pans.  (Note: Only fill to 2/3 the height of the baking pan.)  
8. Bake for 30-35 minutes and then test to see if they are done. The cakes should be set in the center. They are done when a toothpick comes out without wet batter stuck to it. If they are not done, test again in two minutes. If they are still not done, test again in another two minutes.  
9. When the cakes are done, place them on a cooling rack (or just on your counter if you don’t own a cooling rack) to cool to room temperature. Once cooled, they are ready for frosting. The layers can be stored in the fridge, individually wrapped in plastic wrap, for up to 5 days before frosting.  
**Cupcakes:**  
10. Using a heaping ¼ measuring cup, fill the cupcake liners two-thirds to three-quarters full. Do not overfill, or the cupcakes will overflow and sink in the middle. You may have leftover batter. After the first batch bakes, refill the cupcake pan with liners and leftover batter then bake those.  
11. Bake for 22 to 28 minutes. The cupcakes will double in size. They are done when a toothpick comes out with some crumbs but not wet batter stuck to it. If they are not done, test again in two minutes. If they are still not done, test again in another two minutes.  
12. Let the cupcakes cool in the pan then carefully remove them (preferably to a wire rack). Once cooled, they are ready for frosting. The cooled cupcakes can be stored in the fridge, wrapped in plastic wrap, for up to 5 days before frosting.  
**Sheet Cake:**  
12. Pour the batter into the prepped pan. Smooth out as evenly as possible. Tap the pan gently on the counter/table top to remove any large air bubbles. 
13. Bake for 50 minutes to 1 hour, rotating pan halfway through. The cake is done when a toothpick comes out with some crumbs but not wet batter stuck to it. If not done, test again in two minutes. If it is still not done, test again in another two minutes.  
6. Cool & Enjoy
7. Remove from the oven and allow the cake to cool completely in the pan.Once the cake has fully cooled, you can remove the cake from the pan and discard the parchment paper. Frost as desired.  
8. Serving  
9. This cake can easily be cut into bigger or smaller pieces. Cutting it into thirds, lengthwise, and fifths, crosswise, will make 15 generous servings. If you do fourths instead of thirds, you can get 20 servings.
10. Storing  
11. The cooled cake or cake pieces can be stored in the fridge, wrapped in plastic wrap, for up to 5 days before frosting.  
---
> **Baker's Tips:**   
**A metal pan is best. Glass and ceramic conduct heat more slowly than metals (the edges will dry out and the center will take longer to bake fully). If glass/ceramic is all that is available, increase the baking time anywhere from 5-10 minutes.
